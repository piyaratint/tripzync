- **Problem:** budget hotel section leaked through even when user had a loyalty programme selected
- **Root cause 1:** `setGoogleBudgetHotels(budget)` called unconditionally
- **Root cause 2:** `showBudgetMode = noMembership || googleLoyaltyHotels.length === 0`
- **Fix:** `setGoogleBudgetHotels(noMembership ? budget : [])` + `showBudgetMode = noMembership`
